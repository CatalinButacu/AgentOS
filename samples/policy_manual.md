# Information Governance Manual

## Introduction

This manual describes the organization's information governance obligations and internal controls.

## Scope

These policies apply to all employees, contractors, and third-party processors handling company information.

## Definitions

Personal data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

## Data Retention

Personal data is retained for no longer than 24 months from the date of collection.

## Deletion Requests

Data deletion requests submitted by a data subject are honored within 30 days.

## Access Controls

Confidential records are limited to authorized roles on a need-to-know basis.

## Encryption

Data at rest is encrypted using industry standard algorithms and centrally managed keys.

## Incident Response

Security incidents are triaged and reported to the security team within one hour of detection.

## Vendor Management

Third-party vendors are assessed annually for their security and privacy posture.

## Training

All staff complete security awareness training upon hiring and annually thereafter.

## Audit Logging

Access to sensitive systems is logged and reviewed on a monthly basis.

## Backups

Critical systems are backed up daily and restore procedures are tested quarterly.

## Business Continuity

Recovery objectives are defined for each critical service and reviewed yearly.

## Physical Security

Server rooms are protected by badge access and continuous video monitoring.

## Acceptable Use

Company systems may be used only for authorized business purposes.
